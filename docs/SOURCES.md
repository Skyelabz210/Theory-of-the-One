## Established literature

- [Euclid, Elements, Book VII, Heath edition via Perseus](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Euc.+7&fromdoc=Perseus%3Atext%3A1999.01.0086): definitions of unit, multitude and measurement; propositions 1–2 on common measures.
- [Omondi and Premkumar, Residue Number Systems](https://www.worldscientific.com/doi/10.1142/p523): mathematical fundamentals and implementations of arithmetic, including comparison, scaling and division.
- [Kornerup and Matula, Modular arithmetic and residue number systems](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/finite-precision-number-systems-and-arithmetic/modular-arithmetic-and-residue-number-systems/6B9874E48CD720F2B47F424D4B0767B4): parallel channel arithmetic and the distinct difficulty of division and comparisons.
- [Chervyakov et al., high-speed RNS division](https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics8030261): an example of prior algorithmic work. Abstract/search content was consulted; this seed does not claim a full implementation comparison.

The research search was targeted to foundational framing. It was not an exhaustive novelty or priority search, and no private research text was submitted in public search queries.
